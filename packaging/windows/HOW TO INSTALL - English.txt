WATCHROOM - install it and host a watch party (Windows)
=======================================================

Watchroom plays a video from YOUR computer so you and your friends can watch it
together, in sync, with chat. Only you (the host) install it. Friends install
nothing: they open the link you send them in a browser (computer or phone).

What you need
- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
- About 700 MB of free disk space. (Watchroom also keeps a cache of prepared
  video, up to 10 GB, and cleans it up by itself.)
- Internet. Your UPLOAD speed decides how good the picture can be for your
  friends (check it at fast.com -> "Show more info" -> Upload).


1. INSTALL
----------
1. Double-click "Watchroom-Setup-0.2.0.exe".
   (If you received a .zip file: right-click it -> "Extract All..." -> Extract,
   then double-click the .exe inside the new folder.)

2. Windows may show a blue window "Windows protected your PC".
   This is normal for small apps without a paid signing certificate.
   Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

3. Installation takes a few seconds. It asks no questions and needs no
   administrator password. Watchroom opens by itself, and a "Watchroom"
   shortcut appears on the Desktop and in the Start menu.


2. HOST A WATCH PARTY
---------------------
1. Open Watchroom. Click "Open video..." and choose a video file
   (MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, WebM and more), or drag the file onto the window.
   For a YouTube video: paste its link into the box "Paste a YouTube link"
   (under the button) and click "Watch". YouTube videos don't use your
   internet upload: everyone streams them from YouTube.

2. Wait until the bottom bar says "Link ready". The first time this can take
   up to a minute.

3. Click "Invite" (blue button, top right) and then "Copy".
   Send the copied link to your friends (Telegram, WhatsApp, ...).

4. Your friends open the link in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, type a name
   and click "Join". That's all.

5. Anyone can play, pause and jump in the video; everybody stays in sync.
   The chat is on the right. Press F for full screen.

6. If Windows asks "Windows Defender Firewall has blocked some features of
   this app" for Watchroom: tick "Private networks" and click "Allow access".
   This lets video go directly to your friends (faster). If you don't allow
   it, everything still works, just through the internet link.

Keep Watchroom open and the computer awake while you watch.
The link is new every time you start Watchroom - send the new one.


3. TIPS
-------
- Quality: every friend chooses their own quality (button at the bottom right
  of the video) or leaves it on "Auto". If the video stutters for everyone,
  your upload speed is the limit: ask friends to choose 480p or 360p.
- The "Room" tab (right side) shows how each friend is connected, and lets
  you lock the room, set a password, or remove someone.
- Only the video you open is shared. Your other files are never visible
  to anyone.
- Next time, the home screen shows "Continue watching": one click opens the
  video again where you stopped.


4. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------
- "Link unavailable" / the link doesn't open: check that this computer is
  online, then click "Invite" -> "Try again". Wait until "Link ready".
- Friends see a spinning circle after jumping to a new place in the video:
  give it a few seconds, the computer is preparing that part.
- The picture stutters: ask friends to choose a lower quality.
- Send the log to whoever helps you: press the Alt key to show the menu,
  then Help -> Open Log Folder, and send the file "watchroom.log".


UNINSTALL
---------
Windows Settings -> Apps -> Installed apps -> Watchroom -> Uninstall.
